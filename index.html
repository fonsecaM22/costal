Our Story

One griddle, a salt breeze, and a very long coastline.

Coastal Créperie began as a single crêpe pan at a Sunday market in Redondo Beach. Today we follow the shore from Malibu to Long Beach, batter rested overnight, lemons pressed each morning, matcha whisked to order.

Everything is made in front of you, in the two minutes it takes a crepe to turn golden.
